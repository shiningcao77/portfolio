FIG. 01 — HEALTHCARE

MyChart

Bringing clarity and calm to patient healthcare experiences — a self-initiated redesign of the MyChart app, focused on making health information easy to find and act on.

Role Passion Project
Sector Healthcare
Method Mixed research + redesign

FIG. 02 — PROBLEM STATEMENT

When a health app adds to the stress

  • 01 Information overload — too much on screen, with no clear hierarchy.
  • 02 Inefficient navigation, making key actions hard to find.
  • 03 Emotional stress during use, at a moment when users are already anxious about their health.

Goal — create a calm, human-centered interface that makes health data easy to access, and emotions easier to manage.

FIG. 03 — RESEARCH

Mixed-method research

A combination of app store review mining and heuristic evaluation surfaced consistent, specific complaints: patients couldn't find past or upcoming appointments, couldn't request medication refills without calling in, and were bounced out to an external website just to reach a clinic.

FIG. 04 — DESIGN CHALLENGE

How might we redesign MyChart to help patients feel informed and reassured — not overwhelmed?

Three principles guided every screen from here on: clarity — simplify navigation and highlight what matters most; control — enable quick actions like booking, messaging, and finding results; and empathy — keep the experience human-centered throughout.

FIG. 05 — IDEATION, SYSTEM & VISUAL DESIGN

Calm hierarchy, visible actions

Early sketches focused on reorganizing navigation and reducing cognitive friction, grouping related actions and surfacing the ones patients need most. A dedicated design system — primary, secondary, and contrast colors, background, and a clear type scale — kept the final interface consistent and calm.

FIG. 06 — IMPACT & TAKEAWAYS

What changed, and what I learned

  • Clearer navigation reduced cognitive load and boosted overall usability.
  • A calmer experience builds patient confidence and encourages regular use of digital health tools.
  • Streamlined communication means fewer misunderstandings and quicker responses.
  • Better self-service increases patient autonomy and reduces clinic workload.
  • Empathy has to drive design decisions in healthcare — small UI improvements carry outsized emotional weight.
