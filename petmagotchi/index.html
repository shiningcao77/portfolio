04 / HCI COURSE PROJECT · PET CONNECTION

Petmagotchi

A pet-connection experience for students away from their pets, centered on direct, personal interaction.

UX Researcher & Designer
TIMELINE
August–December 2026
COMPANY/CLIENT
Academic project · HCI course
SKILLS/TOOLS

01 / OVERVIEW

Bring the pet into the conversation.

I designed a pet-connection experience for students whose pets are not with them, bringing talking and viewing the pet into one interaction.

Petmagotchi is a semester-long project for an HCI course, exploring how students can connect with pets while they are apart.

02 / PROBLEM

Students wanted a connection that did not depend on someone else.

Students wanted a direct, personal connection with their pets without depending on family members or a third party to mediate it.

Research foundation

I conducted 6 user interviews and used an affinity diagram to synthesize the findings. A central theme was direct connection: users wanted an individual relationship with their pet, without another person needing to intervene.

03 / SOLUTION

Live talk → keep the pet in view while speaking.

I added Live talk so users could speak while seeing the pet, addressing the separation observed during testing.

04 / IMPACT / PROCESS

Observed behavior became a design change.

Behavior · 3 usability sessions

Users returned to the homepage to record and talk.

Insight · Post-session feedback

They wanted to talk while looking at the pet; separating these activities did not match their expectations.

Decision · Add Live talk

I introduced a talking experience that keeps the pet visible. This is a design response to the evidence, not yet a measured improvement.

05 / OPPORTUNITIES / TARGET USERS

Test the revised interaction with the intended audience.

The primary users are students whose pets are not with them. The next opportunity is to learn how the experience fits their routines while they are apart.

Next, test whether users discover Live talk and can speak while keeping the pet in view without returning to another screen.

06 / REFLECTION

Watch the workaround before asking for a preference.

Returning to the homepage revealed a mismatch between the flow and how users wanted to interact. Their feedback helped explain the behavior and led to Live talk.

Three usability sessions provide directional evidence, not a representative result. The next step is to test the revision with a broader group and check whether the workaround disappears.
